# Feedback and Improvements

## Review Process

I reviewed the five prompt templates for clarity, relevance to personal finance, missing information, and the usefulness of the expected output.

I also considered feedback from a peer review where available.

## Feedback 1 – Prompt Clarity

**Feedback:**  
The prompts were clear, but some instructions could be made more specific.

**Change Made:**  
I separated the task into numbered steps and added an expected output format.

## Feedback 2 – Output Format

**Feedback:**  
The AI should have a consistent structure for presenting its response.

**Change Made:**  
Each prompt now includes headings and a defined output structure.

## Feedback 3 – Financial Context

**Feedback:**  
The prompts should avoid giving recommendations without enough user information.

**Change Made:**  
I added missing-information checks and instructions not to make unsupported assumptions.

## Final Review

After reviewing the prompts, I made the instructions more specific, simplified unnecessary wording, and added limitations where appropriate.

The final templates are designed to be practical for a personal finance management platform while keeping the AI's role focused on analysis and general guidance.
